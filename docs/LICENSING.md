# Licensing Forgit

Forgit uses one codebase with two license choices. There is no community
edition, enterprise edition, feature gate, license key, activation server, or
usage telemetry.

## Open-source option

Use Forgit under the
[GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later](../LICENSE) at no charge.
You may run, inspect, modify, and redistribute it. If you modify Forgit and let
people interact with it over a network, the AGPL requires you to offer those
users the corresponding source code. Read the license for the complete terms.

Companies may use the AGPL option. Payment is not required if its terms work
for the intended use.

## Commercial option

The Forgit Commercial License is **$99 USD per legal entity, per year**. It
allows one entity to use the same software without the AGPL's source-sharing
requirements.

One subscription includes:

- unlimited users, repositories, servers, environments, and installations;
- internal, production, and commercial use;
- private modifications;
- hosting Forgit as a service;
- embedding Forgit in a product; and
- distribution as part of a product or service.

Employees are covered. Contractors working on the entity's behalf are covered.
A separately incorporated parent, subsidiary, sister company, portfolio
company, customer, or affiliate needs its own subscription.

The rights to each version received during a paid subscription continue after
cancellation. Cancellation only ends access to new versions under the
commercial terms. A customer can always use a version under the AGPL instead.

The $99 subscription is a license only. It does not include support, consulting,
hosting, warranties, or a service-level agreement. Those may be offered
separately.

To purchase a commercial license before self-service checkout is available,
contact [The Computer Plumbers](https://thecomputerplumbers.com/#contact).

The complete terms are in [LICENSE-COMMERCIAL.md](../LICENSE-COMMERCIAL.md).

## Why this model

The AGPL keeps a complete open-source path for people and organizations willing
to share changes to networked deployments. The commercial option gives teams a
short, predictable way to keep modifications private. Both choices provide the
same code and the same features.

The price is published and does not depend on seats, servers, revenue, funding,
repository count, or negotiation. The current price may change for future
purchases. A price change does not alter rights already granted for Covered
Software.
